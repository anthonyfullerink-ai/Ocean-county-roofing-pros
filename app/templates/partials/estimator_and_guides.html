Make a Confident, No-Pressure Roofing Decision

Straight answers on repairs vs. replacements, NJ coastal wind insurance claims, and why full tear-offs protect your home's value.

Targeted Repair Makes Sense When

Localized Leak, Flashing, or Pipe Boot Failure

If your roof is under 12–15 years old and leaks are isolated to a cracked plumbing boot, chimney step flashing, or a handful of wind-lifted tabs, an honest targeted repair saves thousands.

Full Replacement Is Smarter When

Widespread Granule Loss, Curling, or Soft Decking

Once shingles turn brittle, lose protective granules in gutters, or show multiple attic moisture points, patching creates recurring leaks. Our 21-point photo audit shows you both options transparently.

Step 1: Photo Documentation

High-Resolution Drone & Crease Evidence

Insurance adjusters look for clean, timestamped evidence of wind-creased tabs, lifted seals, and hail bruising—not vague claims. Every inspection delivers organized digital proof.

Step 2: Scope & Code Alignment

NJ Building Code & Ice/Water Requirements

Your matched NJ licensed contractor prepares an itemized scope covering drip edge, Ice & Water Shield, and underlayment code items so nothing gets missed during claim review.

Why Avoid "Nail-Over" Layers

Hidden Rot & Voided Manufacturer Warranties

Installing new shingles over old ones traps summer heat, hides rotted plywood decking, adds unnecessary weight, and frequently voids top-tier manufacturer wind warranties.

The Full Tear-Off Standard

Inspect Plywood, Waterproof & Ventilate

Vetted partner crews strip down to bare decking, replace any damaged sheathing, lay Ice & Water Shield along eaves/valleys, and verify balanced ridge & soffit airflow.
