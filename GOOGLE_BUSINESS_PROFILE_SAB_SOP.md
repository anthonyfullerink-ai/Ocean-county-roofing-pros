## 1. Why You Do NOT Need a Commercial Storefront

Google's official guidelines explicitly distinguish between **Storefront Businesses** (customers come to you) and **Service Area Businesses (SABs)** (you visit customers at their location). 

Roofing, plumbing, landscaping, and mobile inspection companies are **Service Area Businesses**. Under Google's rules, SABs are **forbidden from displaying a public street address** unless they maintain staffed retail counter hours with permanent outdoor signage.

By choosing **Service Area Business (SAB)**:
1. Your residential address or registered agent address is **completely hidden from Google Maps**.
2. Customers see a red outlined coverage zone covering **Ocean County, NJ** (and specific service cities like Toms River, Brick, Lakewood, Point Pleasant, Jackson).
3. You avoid the risk of competitors reporting an unstaffed building or residential home.
