# Brand Guide: Ocean County Roofing Pros

## 1. Brand Identity & Purpose
- **Brand Name:** Ocean County Roofing Pros
- **Tagline:** *"Jersey Shore's Trusted Roof Repair, Replacement & Storm Response"*
- **Mission:** Deliver peace of mind to coastal New Jersey homeowners through honest inspections, hurricane-rated materials, transparent pricing, and 30-second emergency responsiveness.
- **Core Market:** Ocean County and southern Monmouth County, NJ (high wind, salt air, coastal storm vulnerability).

- **Headings:** Bold, punchy sans-serif (`Inter`, `Montserrat`, or system-ui fallback).

- **Legibility Standard:** High contrast ratios (WCAG AAA) for homeowners viewing mobile screens in direct sunlight.

## 3. Brand Voice & Messaging Matrix

### Voice Pillars:
1. **Locally Rooted & Neighborly:** We live, work, and weather the same nor'easters as our customers.
2. **Authoritative & Reassuring:** We remove the stress of roof leaks, insurance red tape, and unexpected damage.
3. **Transparent & Direct:** Clear timelines, no surprise bills, straightforward recommendations (repair vs. full replace).
4. **Urgent on Leaks:** Roof damage gets worse by the hour; our speed-to-lead and dispatch is unmatched.

## 6. Language & Copywriting Guardrails

### Approved Vocabulary:
- *"Coastal wind rating"*
- *"Architectural shingles"*
- *"Ice & water shield barrier"*
- *"Attic ventilation & moisture check"*
- *"Free 21-point comprehensive inspection"*
- *"Direct insurance adjuster representation"*

### Strictly Forbidden Phrases:
- ❌ *"Cheapest roofer in New Jersey"* (Devalues craftsmanship; invites dispute-prone leads).
- ❌ *"Guaranteed approval on insurance claims"* (Illegal / deceptive insurance phrasing).
- ❌ *"Our crew will arrive in 15 minutes"* (Never make unverified logistical guarantees).
- ❌ *"One size fits all pricing"* (Every roof pitch, tear-off layer, and square footage varies).
